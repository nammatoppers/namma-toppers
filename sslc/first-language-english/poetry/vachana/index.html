Vachana

Karnataka SSLC First Language English chapter notes for the 2026–27 academic year.

Summary

Basavanna contrasts rich people who build physical stone temples for Lord Shiva with poor devotees whose own bodies serve as living temples. He reflects that physical structures may perish over time, but the spiritual devotion of the soul (the moving) remains eternal and indestructible.
