Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Welcome to Namma Toppers (https://nammatoppers.in). We prioritize the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Namma Toppers and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Namma Toppers is a free public educational platform. We do not require account registration or login, and we do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as personal names, phone numbers, or residential addresses on our website.

2. Website Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) (Measurement ID: G-W2W9Q8HR1C) to monitor aggregated traffic patterns, popular class resources, button clicks, and general website usage. GA4 collects non-personal diagnostic data including:

  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Anonymized IP and general location (city/country level)
  • User interaction events (e.g., resource button clicks)

This analytics data is strictly used to improve site performance and optimize educational content navigation.

3. Cookies & Web Beacons

Like any standard website, Namma Toppers uses 'cookies' to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. This information is used to optimize the user experience by customizing web page content based on browser types.

4. Future Advertising Disclosures (Google AdSense)

Namma Toppers plans to partner with third-party advertising vendors, such as Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website after formal review and approval. Third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

5. External & Google Drive Resource Links

Our website provides direct links to educational document files hosted on Google Drive and links to official community platforms (Telegram, WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube). When you click an external resource link, you leave Namma Toppers and are subject to the privacy policies of Google Drive or the respective third-party provider.

6. Children's Information

Protecting children's privacy online is paramount. Namma Toppers does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided PII on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information.

7. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
