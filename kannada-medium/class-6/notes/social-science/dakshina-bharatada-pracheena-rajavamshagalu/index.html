ದಕ್ಷಿಣ ಭಾರತದ ಪ್ರಾಚೀನ ರಾಜವಂಶಗಳು

This chapter explores major ancient dynasties of South India including the Kadambas, Gangas, Chalukyas of Badami, Pallavas, and Rashtrakutas. It highlights their political history, administrative achievements, and magnificent temple architecture.

Quick Revision

  • Mayurasharma founded the Kadamba dynasty with Banavasi as capital.
  • Pulakeshin II was the greatest ruler of Badami Chalukyas.
  • Kailasanatha temple at Ellora built under Rashtrakuta patronage.
  • South Indian dynasties contributed greatly to art, literature, and maritime trade.
