ಪ್ರಭುತ್ವದ ಪ್ರಕಾರಗಳು

This civics chapter explains forms of government such as Monarchy, Dictatorship, and Democracy. It highlights democratic principles, rights and duties of citizens, and representative government mechanisms.

Quick Revision

  • Democracy is government of the people, by the people, for the people.
  • Monarchy places supreme power in a hereditary king or queen.
  • Dictatorship concentrates unchecked authority in one ruler or party.
  • Universal adult suffrage enables citizens to vote and choose representatives.
