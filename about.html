About Namma Toppers

Namma Toppers — "Study Smart. Score Big." is an independent digital educational resource platform created specifically to support Karnataka State Board school students, teachers, and parents.

Notice: Namma Toppers is an independent educational initiative. We do not claim any official government affiliation or recognition from the Karnataka School Examination and Assessment Board (KSEAB) or the Department of School Education.

Our Mission

Our core mission is to make well-organized, high-quality, and accessible academic reference materials freely available to school communities across Karnataka. We believe that structured access to practice materials empowers students to build confidence and perform better in their assessments.

Resources We Provide

Namma Toppers curates and organizes academic materials across multiple categories and mediums:

  • Bilingual Resources (Classes 1–5): Formative Assessment (FA-1, FA-2) and Summative Assessment (SA-1, SA-2) question papers and answer keys.
  • Kannada Medium Resources (Classes 1–9): Subject-wise question papers, evaluation keys, unit tests, and chapter notes.
  • English Medium Resources (Classes 1–9): Practice question papers, model keys, and study notes tailored for English medium learners.
  • SSLC Zone (Class 10 Board Resources): Class 10 board exam preparation materials including previous year papers, model question papers, blueprints, important questions, and revision guides.

Who We Serve

  • Students: Access practice papers for self-evaluation, revision, and board exam preparation.
  • Teachers: Easily locate reference test papers and answer keys to aid classroom evaluation and test creation.
  • Parents: Guide children with organized study materials for daily learning and term assessments.
